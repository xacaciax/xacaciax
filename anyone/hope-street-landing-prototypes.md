# Hope Street Landing Page — 6 Story Prototypes for Review

> **What this is:** Six prototype landing pages, each telling Hope Street's story a different way. We want your gut reaction to how each one *feels*.

## How we got here

We mined three years of our own thinking — the wiki, Notion, Slack, and the fundraising decks — and found that there isn't *one* Hope Street story floating around. There are **six**, all true, each leading with a different heart. [Link to research doc](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1BfQgJaHid7mINiJ7eXzXKeYXR83_XAsTmELPqJLcNEk/edit?tab=t.0)

We turned each into a simple landing-page concept. The goal of this round isn't polish — it's to **feel** what each story is like to scroll through, so we can pick the one (or the blend) we move boldly with for the next six months of fundraising.

## How to give feedback

For each version below, open the prototype and notice: **Where did you lean in? Where did you get confused? Which one would you be proud to put in front of a funder, a superintendent, and a teacher?** Drop comments right on this page.
